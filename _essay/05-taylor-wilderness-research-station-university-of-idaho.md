Maurice Hornocker began conducting mountain lion research with his winter headquarters at Taylor Ranch. He thought that the University of Idaho should purchase the ranch as a wilderness field station. He discussed the plan with several University officials and in 1969, a purchase agreement was reached. The University bought Jess Taylor's Ranch for $100,000 and agreed to allow the Taylor's to maintain a lifetime tenancy on their cabin and use of the airstrip. For several years, a variety of research was conducted in the area, alongside an outfitting business. Several caretakers came and went during this time. Although TWRS had been established in 1972, it wasn't until 1982, when Dr. Ed Krumpe became director of TWRS that the University decided to make it a full time research center. Jim & Holly Akenson were hired by the University to be full-time managers of TWRS [8, 9].
