---

Jess Taylor met Dave Lewis while on a hunt in 1933. They became friends, and Jess returned to the "Lewis Place" in the spring of 1934. He knew he would like to purchase the Lewis Place, but was unsure if Dave would want to sell. To his surprise, Dave agreed and Jess paid him $500 on that trip. The sale was closed in the fall with the purchase of the 65 acre ranch at a total of $1,200 and the promise that Dave Lewis could live there until he passed away. In 1948, Jess and his new wife Dorothy moved to Taylor Ranch and began to make it their home. The first major project they undertook was the constructions of the airstrip which is still actively used today. They also added several cabins, a shed, and a log corral [7]. On the ranch, the Taylors hunted, grew a large garden, and put up hay for their livestock. They ran a thriving hunting camp for 18 years which included formidable clientele such as the Mayo brothers (of the Mayo Clinic) [1]. As time passed, the Taylor's had several caretakers at the ranch and leased out their outfitting business while they spent time at their home in Boise. In the mid-1960's they listed their ranch for sale [8].
