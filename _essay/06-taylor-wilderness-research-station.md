## Taylor Ranch Landing Area

For over 40 years, skilled aviators have used the landing area built by Jess Taylor to transport people and supplies to and from TWRS. In the past, caretakers packed down the snow with snowshoes so the plane could land but ranch managers now use a snowblower (as seen on right) to keep the runway cleared. Every Wednesday, Arnold Aviation flies from Cascade, Idaho across the mountains to TWRS to deliver supplies and mail. Mail has been flown into the Idaho backcountry since 1928. Taylor is the most remote destination that still receives United States Postal Service delivery [10].

## Ranch Managers

Since 1982, the University of Idaho has employed on-site managers to keep Taylor Wilderness Research Station running smoothly. During this time, new buildings have been built, old buildings have been renovated, modern conveniences have been added, and the station has developed into what you see today. Researchers, professors, students, and visitors have been met with warm hospitality during their time spent at TWRS. All of the managers have had a huge hand in ensuring that advancements in technology, research, and educational programming in a dynamic wilderness classroom are being continued. This year-around position is not for the faint of heart. These people are isolated from the outside world yet are connected to the land in a way that many of us will never understand.
